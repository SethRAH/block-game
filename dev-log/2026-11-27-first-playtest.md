# 2026-11-27: First playtest

**Status:** observation only. No code changed.
**Build tested:** endless mode as shipped in [#3](https://github.com/SethRAH/block-game/pull/3), live at https://sethrah.github.io/block-game/
**Source:** the owner's playtest report.

## TL;DR

The game was playtested with its target player, a child. Dragging and
placing pieces felt natural to her, and endless mode kept her playing. She
wanted the score to be bigger and more central. She never used Undo or
"Swap pieces?". She asked about levels at the start.

## What we saw

- **Placement and drag landed.** Picking up, dragging and placing pieces felt
  intuitive and worked well. The core interaction did its job.
- **Score wants more prominence.** She wanted the score bigger and more central
  on the screen.
- **Undo and Swap went unused.** She did not use the Undo button or the
  "Swap pieces?" button at all, even after they were explained.
- **Endless mode is sticky.** She asked about levels at the start, but once she
  was playing endless she just kept going.

## Takeaways

These are signals from one playtest, not decisions. Product and levels planning
is for the team to drive (Plan bot).

1. **Levels look like the right next feature.** She asked for them before she
   started playing. They were already next on the roadmap (see
   [the prototype entry](2026-11-27-block-game-prototype.md#whats-next)).
2. **Revisit score prominence.** Her one direct request was a bigger, more
   central score.
3. **Note Undo and Swap for a future UI pass.** Both went unused. This log
   doesn't say why. Worth keeping in mind when the UI is next reworked.
